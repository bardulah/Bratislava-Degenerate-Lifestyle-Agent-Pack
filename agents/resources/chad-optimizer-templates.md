### Status Key
- **Applied:** Sent, waiting
- **Viewed:** They looked at profile (LinkedIn shows this)
- **Phone:** Phone screen scheduled/completed
- **Interview:** On-site/video interview
- **Rejected:** Closed
- **Offer:** Decision time

### Weekly Review Questions
1. How many total applications this week?
2. How many quality (customized) applications?
3. Any patterns in rejections?
4. What skills keep appearing in desired jobs?
5. Who did I talk to this week?

## 8. Interview Prep Checklist

### 48 Hours Before
- [ ] Research company (recent news, culture)
- [ ] Review job description, match to your experience
- [ ] Prepare 3 questions to ask
- [ ] Plan outfit (clean, appropriate)
- [ ] Test tech if video call

### Day Before
- [ ] Print CV (2 copies) if in-person
- [ ] Plan route + add 30min buffer
- [ ] Prepare STAR stories for common questions
- [ ] Get good sleep

### Day Of
- [ ] Light breakfast (not starving, not stuffed)
- [ ] No coffee overdose (you need calm, not jitters)
- [ ] Arrive 10min early
- [ ] Phone on silent

### After Interview
- [ ] Send thank-you email within 24h
- [ ] Note what went well/poorly for next time
- [ ] Update tracker
- [ ] Continue applying (never stop for one opportunity)
