## Route Templates

### 1. Záhorie Gravel Classic (50km, 300m)
**Best for:** 3-hour gravel ride, intermediate

**Start:** Most SNP
**Route:** Devínska Nová Ves → Stupava → Borinka forest roads → Marianka → Záhorská Bystrica

**Key waypoints:**
- Stupava center: Last reliable water/food
- Borinka forest: Main gravel section, packed sand
- Marianka monastery: Fountain for refill
- Záhorská Bystrica: Hostinec u Medveďa finish

**Conditions:** Drains well except Lozorno section. Rideable 48h after rain on 35mm+ tires.

**GPX creation:** Plot these points in Komoot or Strava route builder:
```
48.1697, 17.1022 (Start - Most SNP)
48.2097, 17.0311 (Devínska)
48.2722, 17.0692 (Stupava)
48.2489, 17.1194 (Borinka entry)
48.2614, 17.1647 (Marianka)
48.2203, 17.0564 (Záhorská Bystrica)
```

### 2. Malé Karpaty MTB Loop (35km, 800m)
**Best for:** Half-day MTB, advanced

**Start:** Koliba parking
**Route:** Kamzík trails → Železná studnička → Biely Kríž → Pekná Cesta descent

**Difficulty:** Blue/Red trails, some technical roots and rocks

**Seasonal notes:**
- Spring: Muddy until late April
- Summer: Dusty, bring extra water
- Autumn: Leaf coverage hides roots
- Winter: Often rideable but slippery

---

### 3. Road Classics Loop (80km, 600m)
**Best for:** Weekend road ride, intermediate+

**Route:** Bratislava → Svätý Jur → Pezinok → Modra → Častá → Bratislava

**Key climbs:**
- Svätý Jur approach: 3km at 5%
- Pezinok to Modra: Rolling

**Group ride:** Wednesday evening departures from Riviéra, 18:00 in summer

## Post-Ride Spots by Trail Area

### Koliba/Kamzík Area
- **Zbojnícka Koliba:** Traditional, good for bad weather, basic food
- **Železná studnička bufet:** Quick beer, casual

### Záhorie
- **Hostinec u Medveďa (Záhorská Bystrica):** Cheap, Slovak pub, don't care about cleats
- **Stupava center:** Multiple options, more polish

### Old Town (after urban rides)
- **Bratislavský Meštiansky Pivovar:** Best beer, pricier
- **Channels (Ventúrska):** Cheap, local, no tourists

### Petržalka/Danube
- **Any Eurovea terrace:** Convenient but touristy
- **Dunajský Pivovar:** Better quality

## Maintenance Contacts

### Budget Shops
- **Cyklocentrum Galéria:** Fair prices, can be slow. Cable job ~15€
- **Cykloveža Nivy:** Basic service cheap and quick

### Specialized
- **Kellys shop:** Brand knowledge, good for Kellys owners
- **Small Petržalka shops:** Quick fixes, variable quality

### Parts Sources
- **Bazos.sk:** Search daily, good deals on used groupsets
- **FB Marketplace:** "Cyklistika Bratislava" groups
- **Decathlon:** Budget tools, Lidl cycling events for cheap accessories

## Weather Resources

### Apps
- **Windy.com:** Best for wind direction and precipitation radar
- **yr.no:** Most accurate for BA specifically
- **Ventusky:** Good alternative radar

### Microclimate Rules
- Záhorie gets wind when Karpaty is calm
- Fog in valleys clears by 10AM usually
- Danube corridor funnels south wind
- If Austria storms, give it 2-3 hours to reach BA
