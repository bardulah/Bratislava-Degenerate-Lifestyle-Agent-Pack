# Psychedelic Integration - Structured Journal Prompts

## How to Use This Document

- Don't do all prompts at once. Pick 1-3 per session.
- Write freely — don't edit or judge as you go
- Return to prompts multiple times as your understanding evolves
- Some prompts work better immediately post-experience, others need time

---

## Immediate (0-24 hours)

### Capturing the Raw Experience

1. **Describe what happened in present tense.**
   Write as if it's happening now. "I am seeing... I feel... My body is..."
   (This preserves pre-verbal impressions before the narrative mind takes over)

2. **What did you see, hear, feel physically?**
   Focus on sensory experience before meaning-making.

3. **What was the most intense moment?**
   Where were you mentally and physically when it peaked?

4. **What surprised you?**
   What showed up that you didn't expect?

5. **What image, phrase, or feeling keeps returning?**
   Even if it doesn't make sense yet, capture it.

---

## Short-Term (1-7 days)

### Processing Content

6. **If your experience had a message, what would it be in one sentence?**
   Don't overthink — write the first thing that comes.

7. **What did you learn about yourself that you didn't know before?**
   Or knew but weren't fully acknowledging?

8. **What emotion were you avoiding that came up?**
   Grief, anger, fear, joy, guilt? Name it specifically.

9. **Who or what did you think about during the experience?**
   People, relationships, memories — what surfaced and why might that be?

10. **What part of your life looks different now?**
    What previously seemed fine that now seems misaligned? What seemed bad that now seems okay?

### Challenging Material

11. **If a difficult moment had something to teach me, what was it?**
    Assume it was purposeful — what's the lesson?

12. **What part of me was I shown that I usually hide?**
    This is shadow work. What got revealed?

13. **What was I resisting? Why?**
    What did my mind/body try to avoid or push away?

14. **Write a dialogue with the difficult part.**
    "I am scared because..." "You make me feel..." Let both sides speak.

### Body Wisdom

15. **Where do I still feel the experience in my body?**
    Any tension, openness, warmth, constriction? What does that area hold?

16. **What does my body know that my mind is still figuring out?**

---

## Long-Term (1-4 weeks)

### Values Clarification

17. **What matters more to me now than it did before?**
    List 3-5 things. Be specific.

18. **What matters less?**
    What dropped in importance? Why?

19. **If I lived fully aligned with what I saw, what would change?**
    Describe a day in that life.

20. **What am I tolerating that I saw I shouldn't be?**
    Job? Relationship? Habit? Living situation?

### Insight to Action

21. **What is ONE thing I can change this week based on what I learned?**
    Not five things. One concrete action.

22. **What habit or pattern am I being called to break?**
    What's the first small step to interrupt it?

23. **What habit or practice am I being called to start?**
    What's the smallest version I can do tomorrow?

24. **What conversation do I need to have that I've been avoiding?**
    With who? About what? When?

25. **What would I do if I actually believed what I experienced?**
    If it was real, what follows logically?

## Shadow Work Prompts

### For Difficult Emotions That Surfaced

26. **The anger/fear/grief I felt was about...**
    Let it be specific. Name names, events, specifics.

27. **When did I first learn to suppress this feeling?**
    Trace it back. How old were you?

28. **What was I protecting by keeping this hidden?**
    There was a reason. What was the protective function?

29. **What does this feeling need from me now?**
    Acknowledgment? Expression? Action? Just to be felt?

30. **Write a letter to your younger self who first felt this.**
    What do they need to hear?

### For Parts Work (IFS-style)

31. **What part of me showed up during the experience?**
    Give it a name or image. "The critic," "The scared child," "The controller"

32. **What does this part want?**
    What's it trying to achieve or protect?

33. **What is this part afraid would happen if it stopped doing its job?**

34. **How can I honor this part's intention while changing its strategy?**

---

## Relational Insights

35. **What patterns in my relationships did I see?**
    Describe the pattern specifically. When does it show up?

36. **What do I need in relationship that I haven't been asking for?**

37. **What have I been doing in relationships that doesn't serve me or others?**

38. **Who do I need to forgive? (Including myself)**
    What would forgiveness look like as an action, not just a feeling?

39. **Who do I need to appreciate or thank?**
    Could you tell them?

---

## Long-Term Integration Check-ins

### Monthly Review (do at 1 month, 3 months, 6 months)

40. **What insights have stuck? What faded?**
    Why did some integrate and others not?

41. **What have I actually changed in my life since the experience?**
    List concrete actions taken.

42. **What did I intend to change but haven't?**
    What's blocking it? Fear? Logistics? Forgot?

43. **Where am I still the same person I was trying to change?**
    No judgment — just honest inventory.

44. **What support do I need to continue this work?**
    Therapy? Community? Practices? Another journey?

45. **How can I re-access this wisdom without substances?**
    Meditation? Nature? Breathwork? Art?

---

## Re-Entry Questions

### For Returning to "Normal" Life

46. **How do I talk about this to people who don't understand?**
    Who is safe to share with? What's appropriate?

47. **What parts of my old life feel hard to return to?**
    What specifically feels off now?

48. **What spiritual bypassing might I be doing?**
    Am I using insights to avoid practical life issues?

49. **What is the difference between a real change and a post-trip glow?**
    How will I know in 30 days if this was real?

50. **What daily practice will keep this work alive?**
    Pick one thing you'll do daily to stay connected to what you learned.

## Quick Integration Exercises

### The One-Page Integration
After any experience, fill out this single page:

```
THE EXPERIENCE IN ONE SENTENCE:

WHAT I FELT (emotionally):

WHAT I SAW (insights):

WHAT SURPRISED ME:

WHAT I'M CALLED TO CHANGE:

ONE ACTION I'LL TAKE THIS WEEK:

WHAT I NEED SUPPORT WITH:
```

### Daily Integration Check-In (5 min)
For the first week post-experience:

1. What do I remember today about the experience?
2. How is it affecting my mood/choices today?
3. One thing I'll do today aligned with what I learned:

## When to Stop Journaling and Get Help

If after 2+ weeks you experience:
- Persistent anxiety or panic
- Can't sleep or function
- Intrusive disturbing thoughts
- Feeling disconnected from reality
- Depression that isn't lifting

Seek professional support:
- MAPS Integration List: maps.org/integration
- Fireside Project: 62-FIRESIDE (psychedelic peer support)
- ICEERS Integration Support
- Local psychedelic-informed therapist

Journaling is powerful but has limits. Some material needs professional containment.
